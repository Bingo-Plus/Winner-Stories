## How to Verify a Published Story

Before relying on a published winner story:

1. Check the original publication.
2. Review the publication date.
3. Distinguish verified facts from personal experiences.
4. Look for clear source attribution.
5. Avoid assuming that individual outcomes are typical.
6. Respect personal privacy and publication permissions.

Individual gaming outcomes are not representative of future results.
